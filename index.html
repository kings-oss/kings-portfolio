About Me

⚖️ Law & Research

I’m a law student and legal researcher with a keen interest in human rights law and commercial law — two areas that reveal so much about justice, power, and people. I currently serve as the Editor-in-Chief of the Kisii University Law Review and Editor in Charge of LOG – LSAK, roles that let me guide meaningful legal conversations and refine the craft of writing.

For me, law isn’t just about statutes or cases — it’s a living dialogue between justice and humanity. My ambition is to grow into a versatile legal authority who not only interprets the law but helps reshape it for a fairer, more conscious world.

🎙️ Creative & Voice Work

Beyond the courtroom and the text, I’m a voice-over artist, writer, graphic designer, poet, and award-winning public speaker — expressions of my belief that creativity and intellect are inseparable.

I’m drawn to the sound, rhythm, and visual power of words. Whether I’m voicing a story, crafting an article, or designing a piece, I’m guided by one goal: to make ideas resonate — not just in the mind, but in the heart.
